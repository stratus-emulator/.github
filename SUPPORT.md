# Getting help

- **Questions** — ask in [Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/stratus-emulator/stratus/discussions/categories/q-a);
  search there first, someone may have asked already.
- **Ideas** — share them in [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/stratus-emulator/stratus/discussions/categories/ideas)
  before they become a feature request.
- **Bugs** — open an [issue](https://github.com/stratus-emulator/stratus/issues/new/choose) with the bug report form.
- **Security problems** — follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue or discussion.

## Supporting the project

stratus is free and open source. If it saves you time, you can
[sponsor its maintainer](https://github.com/sponsors/ODudek), star the repository, or tell others about it.
